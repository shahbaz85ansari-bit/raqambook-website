Delete your account and data

Choti si baat (Hinglish mein): App ke andar Profile → Delete Account se aap apna account aur sara data hata sakte hain. App na ho to email bhejein, hum 30 din mein hata denge.

This page is for Raqam Book (Google Play app by Raqam Book). It explains how to delete your account and the data kept in it.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  • Open Raqam Book and log in.
  • Go to Profile and tap Delete Account.
  • Read what will be deleted, tick "I understand", and enter your app PIN.
  • Tap the delete button. Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: Ask us by e-mail

If you cannot open the app (for example you lost your phone or uninstalled it), write to raqambook@gmail.com from the e-mail linked to your account, or tell us the mobile number you log in with. Write "Delete my Raqam Book account" in the subject. We will delete your account and its data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your login and profile (mobile number, or Google account name and e-mail).
  • Your customers (names and mobile numbers), entries, notes and attached photos.
  • Your chips, Income & Expense entries, Auto Repeat entries and reminders.
  • Your staff, salary entries and attendance.
  • Your app settings and PIN.

The data is removed from our cloud storage (Google Firebase) and from the phone you delete from. This cannot be undone.

What is not deleted

  • Backups in your own Google Drive stay there, because they are in your Google account. You can delete them yourself from Google Drive.
  • Copies of the app's data on your other phones are removed the next time you log in, because the account no longer exists.
  • Messages you already sent to customers on WhatsApp or SMS, and PDF or Excel files you already shared, are outside the app and are not deleted.

How long we keep data

After deletion we keep nothing for ourselves. Cloud copies are fully removed within 30 days at most.
