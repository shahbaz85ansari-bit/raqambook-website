Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Choti si baat (Hinglish mein): Raqam Book free chalta hai. Sirf "Go Premium" (ads hatane wala monthly plan) paise ka hai, aur uska paisa Google Play leta hai. Isliye band karna aur refund Google Play se hota hai. Koi dikkat ho to humein likhein, hum madad karenge. Poori baat neeche English mein hai.

This page explains what you pay for in Raqam Book, how to cancel, and how to ask for a refund.

1. What costs money

  • The app is free to download and use. Nothing is charged for the free plan.
  • Go Premium is the only paid item. It is a monthly subscription that removes ads. The price is shown in Google Play before you buy.

2. Who takes the payment

Go Premium is sold through Google Play. Google Play collects the payment, renews it every month, and keeps the payment records. We never see or keep your card, UPI or bank details.

3. Renewal

The subscription renews by itself every month until you cancel it. Google Play charges you again on the same date each month.

4. How to cancel

  • Open the Google Play Store app on your phone.
  • Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  • Choose Raqam Book and tap Cancel subscription.

After you cancel, you are not charged again. You keep Premium until the end of the month you have already paid for.

Please note: deleting your Raqam Book account, or uninstalling the app, does not cancel the subscription. Google Play keeps charging until you cancel it in Google Play.

5. Refunds

  • Refunds for Google Play purchases are decided by Google under Google Play's own refund rules, which can change. At the time of writing, Google usually lets you ask for a refund soon after buying. You can ask in Google Play under your order history, or on Google's help page.
  • We cannot refund a Google Play payment ourselves, because we do not hold the money.
  • A cancelled month that has already been paid is normally not refunded for the days left in it.
  • Where the law gives you a right to a refund, that right stays.

6. When something goes wrong

Please write to us if:

  • you were charged twice for the same month,
  • you paid but ads did not go away or Premium did not start, or
  • you see a charge you did not make.

Send us your Google Play order number (it looks like GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567) and a short note of what happened. We will look at it and reply within 7 days. We will fix the problem in the app if the fault is ours, and we will help you ask Google for a refund where that is the right way. We cannot promise a refund, because Google decides it.

Please write to us first before you dispute a charge with your bank or card company, so we can try to sort it out quickly.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown on this page with its date. If a price or plan changes, Google Play will show it to you before it applies to you.
