Last updated: 19 November 2026

Raqam Book ("the app", "we") is a digital ledger for shops and homes. This policy says what the app keeps, why, where it is stored, who else can see it, and how you can delete it. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Raqam Book" mean Shahbaz Ansari, who runs the app (address in section 12).

1. What the app keeps

  • Your login: your mobile number, or your Google account name and email address.
  • What you write in the app: your business details; your customers (names and mobile numbers); entries, amounts, notes and photos you attach; chips (Customers, Self Business, Income & Expense, Staff Salary and your own); staff with their salary and attendance; Auto Repeat entries and reminders; and your app settings.
  • Information about other people: your customers and staff are other people. If you save their details, you confirm that you have the right to do so and that they know you keep them in the app.
  • Phone contacts: if you pick a customer or a staff member from your phone contacts, only that contact's name and number are taken. The app does not read or upload your whole contact list.

2. Why we keep it

Only to run the app for you: to show your books, to keep them the same on all your phones, to make backups, statements and reports, and to remind you. We do not sell your data.

3. Where it is stored

  • On your phone.
  • In your own account in Google Firebase (the cloud), so your books are there when you log in on another phone. Only your login can open it.
  • If you turn on backup, an encrypted copy is saved in your own Google Drive.
  • Google may store and process this data on servers in India or in other countries. Those countries have their own data laws. By using the app you agree to this.

4. Who else can see it

No one, except the Google services that run the app:

  • Firebase (login and storage).
  • Google Drive (your backups).
  • Google Play (app updates and the Premium subscription).
  • Google AdMob (ads). Ads may use your phone's advertising ID and similar device information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Messages you send to your customers on WhatsApp, SMS or e-mail go from your own phone, using the apps you choose. We do not send them.

We may also have to share data when the law requires it, for example on a valid order of a court or a government authority.

5. Phone permissions

  • Microphone: only while you speak an entry. The voice is turned into text by your phone's speech service; the app does not keep the recording.
  • Camera and photos: only to attach a photo or bill you choose.
  • Contacts: only when you pick a customer or a staff member from them.
  • Notifications and alarms: for your reminders and Auto Repeat.
  • Fingerprint / face: for the app lock. The app never receives your fingerprint; the phone only says yes or no.
  • Internet: to log in, save to the cloud, make backups and show ads.

6. Keeping it safe

Your data is kept under your own login. It is encrypted while it travels between the app and the cloud, and backups are encrypted. You can add a PIN and an app lock. We try to follow the reasonable security practices expected under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. No system is perfectly safe, so please keep your phone, OTP and PIN to yourself. If a data breach affects you, we will act as the law requires.

7. How long we keep data, and deleting it

We keep your data for as long as your account is open, so the app can work for you. When you delete your account, the data is removed from our cloud storage and from the phone; cloud copies are fully gone within 30 days at most. We do not keep a copy for ourselves. Records of Premium payments are kept by Google Play under Google's own rules.

Full steps: raqambook.com/delete-account.html

  • In the app: Profile → Delete Account. It deletes your account and all its data from the cloud and from the phone. This cannot be undone.
  • Backups in your own Google Drive are not deleted by the app; you can delete them yourself from Google Drive.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to the e-mail address below. Give the mobile number or Google account you use in the app, and we will delete it within 30 days.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can see, correct and delete your entries, customers, staff and settings inside the app at any time.
  • You can delete your whole account (see section 7).
  • You can take back a phone permission (contacts, camera, microphone, notifications) any time in your phone's Settings. The feature that needs it will then stop working.
  • For anything else, or if you want help with your data, write to the e-mail address in section 12. We try to reply within 30 days.

9. Our website and other links

This website is a plain information page. It does not use cookies or analytics. The page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep its own server logs (for example IP addresses). The app and this website may show or link to other companies' sites and services (for example Google and Google Play). Their own privacy policies apply to them, and we are not responsible for them.

10. Children

Raqam Book is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data of children.

11. Changes to this policy and to our business

If this policy changes, the new one will be shown here and in the app, with its date. If our business is ever sold, merged or handed over to someone else, your data may move with it, and the new owner must follow this policy. We will tell you in the app first.
