Privacy Policy

Choti si baat (Hinglish mein): Aapka data aapke apne login ke andar rehta hai. Hum aapka data bechte nahi. Aap kabhi bhi app mein Profile → Delete Account se apna sab data hata sakte hain. Poori policy neeche English mein hai.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Raqam Book ("the app", "we") is a digital ledger for shops and homes. This policy says what the app keeps, why, where it is stored, who else can see it, and how you can delete it.

1. What the app keeps

  • Your login: your mobile number, or your Google account name and email address.
  • What you write in the app: your business details; your customers (names and mobile numbers); entries, amounts, notes and photos you attach; chips (Customers, Self Business, Income & Expense, Staff Salary and your own); staff with their salary and attendance; Auto Repeat entries and reminders; and your app settings.
  • Phone contacts: if you pick a customer or a staff member from your phone contacts, only that contact's name and number are taken. The app does not read or upload your whole contact list.

2. Why we keep it

Only to run the app for you: to show your books, to keep them the same on all your phones, to make backups, statements and reports, and to remind you. We do not sell your data.

3. Where it is stored

  • On your phone.
  • In your own account in Google Firebase (the cloud), so your books are there when you log in on another phone. Only your login can open it.
  • If you turn on backup, an encrypted copy is saved in your own Google Drive.

4. Who else can see it

No one, except the Google services that run the app:

  • Firebase (login and storage).
  • Google Drive (your backups).
  • Google Play (app updates and the Premium subscription).
  • Google AdMob (ads). Ads may use your phone's advertising ID and similar device information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Messages you send to your customers on WhatsApp, SMS or e-mail go from your own phone, using the apps you choose. We do not send them.

5. Phone permissions

  • Microphone: only while you speak an entry. The voice is turned into text by your phone's speech service; the app does not keep the recording.
  • Camera and photos: only to attach a photo or bill you choose.
  • Contacts: only when you pick a customer or a staff member from them.
  • Notifications and alarms: for your reminders and Auto Repeat.
  • Fingerprint / face: for the app lock. The app never receives your fingerprint; the phone only says yes or no.
  • Internet: to log in, save to the cloud, make backups and show ads.

6. Keeping it safe

Your data is kept under your own login. You can add a PIN and an app lock, and backups are encrypted. No system is perfectly safe, so please keep your phone and PIN to yourself.

7. Deleting your data and your account

  • In the app: Profile → Delete Account. It deletes your account and all its data from the cloud and from the phone. This cannot be undone.
  • Backups in your own Google Drive are not deleted by the app; you can delete them yourself from Google Drive.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to the e-mail address below. Give the mobile number or Google account you use in the app, and we will delete it within 30 days.

8. Children

Raqam Book is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new one will be shown here and in the app, with its date.
